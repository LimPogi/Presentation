The business and the message.

The business

Ember & Oat Coffee is a coffee shop brand concept with a warm, inviting identity. Its intended audience can be described as coffee drinkers who appreciate a comfortable café atmosphere, approachable branding, and an easy way to discover the brand's products.

Audience note: Early-rising professionals, work-from-home remote workers, and local residents seeking premium, small-batch craft coffee delivered directly to their doorstep before their workday begins.

A clear next step

The coded CTA flyer introduces the brand, highlights its featured message or offer, and guides viewers toward one clear action.

2 of 5 Brand and CTA presentationEmber & Oat Coffee

The flyer, logo, and icons.

Logo

  • Highlights our Flagship Product & Brand Story: Features a steaming coffee cup paired with oat stalk accents to directly communicate our signature coffee and oat offerings.
  • Represents Quality & Comfort: The warm circular seal, coffee bean elements, and cozy serif lettering embody freshly brewed, artisanal coffee made with care.
  • Warm & Professional Aesthetic: Uses inviting earth tones to establish a modern, trustworthy, and handcrafted micro-roastery identity.

Iconography

  • Feature Highlights: Uses clean line-art icons such as a sun (daily morning roasts), a lightning bolt (30-minute fast delivery), and a dollar sign (craft quality starting at $3) to quickly communicate key customer benefits.
  • Interactive & Promo Elements: Incorporates copy/checkmark indicators to highlight promo code activation (FIRSTSIP) and guide quick engagement.
  • Seamless Ordering Guidance: Integrates clear contact symbols and a scan-to-order QR code to streamline mobile navigation and direct online purchases.
